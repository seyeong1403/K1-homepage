／ 01  CEO Message

A site always begins
with the unexpected

K1 is the first company in Korea to establish a turnkey commissioning system — a commissioning specialist for the shipbuilding, offshore and energy industries.

Commissioning is the last process of a project, but preparing for it must begin the day the project starts.

That is why we join the site from the earliest project stage, verifying commissioning readiness in advance, carrying out system-by-system commissioning, and staying with our clients through final completion and handover.

Drawing on experience accumulated in the field, we have consolidated a more systematic way of commissioning into a single proprietary framework — our turnkey commissioning method. Moving beyond the conventional manpower-centred approach, K1 provides an integrated commissioning service that manages every process within one system.

Our competitiveness lies not in headcount alone, but in experience that understands the site, an organisation that connects technologies, and the execution to carry a project through to completion.

K1 will continue to grow beyond Korea into a trusted commissioning partner for the global shipbuilding, offshore and energy industries.

K1's goal is not to become a large company, but a company clients come back to.

K1 Co., Ltd. Chief Executive Officer Sang-woon Kang

Our Capabilities
01

Electrical Commissioning

Securing the stability and reliability of power systems through testing and grid integration of generators, transformers, switchboards and protection relays

02

Instrumentation Commissioning

Completing precision control systems through DCS/PLC loop checks, instrument calibration, and interlock and trip testing

03

Mechanical Commissioning

Establishing optimal operating conditions through performance testing across all mechanical equipment

04

HVAC Commissioning

Securing operating efficiency and environmental stability through commissioning of AHU, FCU, chiller and compressor systems

05

Test Equipment

Advanced testing with specialist equipment — relay testers, insulation and earthing testers, and precision calibrators

Quality & Safety
