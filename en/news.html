K1 Mascot

KIO KIO

KIO : K1 + ONE & ON

KIO stands for K1's vision of “KOREA ONLY ONE” and for the essence of commissioning — that “K1 completes the system in the ON state.” Like the orca that leads the sea with outstanding intelligence and strength, K1's mascot KIO embodies the expertise built on offshore sites, strong collaboration, dependable leadership, and the power of execution that carries a project to success.

Kirangi

Kirangi is K1 OCEAN's mascot, bright and friendly in energy with a strong spirit of challenge. It stands for warm connection, an innovative vision for the future of the marine industry, and the drive to open up new seas.
