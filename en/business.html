Commissioning

Commissioning — the technology that brings systems to life

We deliver one-stop commissioning across electrical, mechanical, instrumentation, HVAC and navigation & telecom.

01

Electrical Commissioning

Electrical
  • Integrated commissioning of high-voltage and power systems
  • Synchronizing / load test / PMS verification
  • DP & FMEA commissioning
02

Instrumentation Commissioning

Instrumentation & Control
  • Calibration & loop check
  • Verification of control valves, transmitters and measurement systems
  • Control logic verification based on C&E testing
03

Mechanical Commissioning

Mechanical
  • Testing of rotating and static equipment — pumps, compressors, valves
  • Verification of equipment function and operating condition
  • Confirmation of system stability and performance
04

Hull Outfitting Commissioning

Hull Outfitting Commissioning
  • Commissioning of cargo oil and ballast systems
  • Function checks of deck machinery, fire-fighting and life-saving equipment
  • Verification of hull outfitting systems and pipe cleanliness
05

Navigation & Telecom Commissioning

Navigation & Telecom
  • Interface testing of navigation and communication systems
  • Function verification of GPS, radar and GMDSS equipment
  • Integrated interface verification
06

HVAC Commissioning

HVAC
  • Performance verification of heating, cooling and ventilation systems
  • Function testing of ducting and HVAC systems
  • Confirmation of indoor environment control and operating stability
Green & Decarbonization Solutions

Technical Support — Green & Decarbonization

From vessel systems engineering to eco-friendly propulsion, our support reaches beyond commissioning.

01

FGSS

LNG Fuel Gas Supply System
  • Technical support for LNG fuel gas supply systems
  • Verification of storage, transfer, vaporisation and pressure control systems
  • Review of alarms, interlocks and safety systems
02

ALS

Air Lubrication System
  • Technical support for hull air lubrication systems
  • Verification of air supply units and piping systems
  • Review of operating conditions and control systems
03

Wind Challenger

Wind-Assisted Propulsion System
  • Technical support for wind-assisted propulsion systems
  • Verification of sail drive and control status
  • Review of voyage conditions and safety interlocks
04

LNG Re-Gas System

LNG Regasification System
  • Technical support for LNG regasification systems
  • Verification of vaporisation, pressure and temperature control systems
  • Review of valve and safety system operation
05

Gas Turbine

Gas Turbine System
  • Technical support for gas turbine operation
  • Verification of fuel supply and start-up conditions
  • Review of control and protection systems
06

WTMS

Water Treatment Monitoring System
  • Technical support for water treatment monitoring systems
  • Verification of water quality and measurement data
  • Review of alarms and operating status
07

BWTS

Ballast Water Treatment System
  • Technical support for ballast water treatment systems
  • Verification of treatment equipment and piping systems
  • Review of alarms, controls and discharge conditions
08

SOx-Scrubber

Sulfur Oxides Scrubber System
  • Technical support for sulphur oxide reduction systems
  • Verification of wash water and exhaust gas systems
  • Review of monitoring equipment operating status
09

SCR

Selective Catalytic Reduction
  • Technical support for nitrogen oxide reduction systems
  • Verification of reductant supply and catalyst systems
  • Review of exhaust temperature, alarm and control conditions
10

AMP

Alternative Maritime Power
  • Technical support for alternative maritime power systems
  • Verification of voltage, frequency and synchronisation status
  • Review of protection devices and power connection status
Maintenance

Maintenance — the management that keeps operation steady

We provide maintenance support that keeps operation stable and equipment performing, through condition checks and responses matched to the situation on site.

01

O&M Support

Operation & Maintenance Support
  • Support for equipment operation and maintenance work
  • Verification of operating conditions, equipment condition and system-by-system operation
  • On-site technical support for stable plant operation
02

After-Sales Support

After Service Support
  • Review of abnormalities arising during equipment operation
  • Cause identification and response guidance based on site conditions
03

Control & Safety Logic Check

Control & Safety Logic Check
  • Verification of alarms, interlocks and protection functions against operating conditions
  • Checking signal interfaces and operating conditions between control systems and field equipment
  • Confirming that equipment stops or is controlled safely under abnormal operation
04

Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting
  • Review of equipment faults and system error causes
  • Comprehensive checks of site operating conditions, control signals and equipment status
  • Technical response support to resolve the problem
05

Maintenance History Management

Maintenance History Management
  • Recording inspection results, faults and corrective actions
  • Managing maintenance records and confirming follow-up actions
  • Securing continuous operating stability by tracking equipment condition
