／ 01  Recruitment

Recruitment Guide

A company proven by trust rather than size. K1 grows with people who hold to principle, collaborate flexibly and stay responsible to the end.

Who We Look For
01
People who hold to principleTrust

They judge on clear standards and evidence, and keep to the promised quality and schedule. Even small standards are never waved through.

02
People who build togetherCollaboration

They communicate openly so that clients, partners and colleagues move in the same direction, creating better outcomes together.

03
People who see it throughResponsibility

They value the finish over the start, watching for unseen risks to protect the quality of the result.

Hiring Process

The process is transparent and prompt, centred on each applicant's capability and fit for the field.

STEP 01

Application Review

We review the CV and career statement for job fit and core capability.

STEP 02

Working Interview

Field and technical managers explore job capability and project experience in depth.

STEP 03

Final Interview

We review values, collaborative attitude and organisational fit as a whole.

STEP 04

Joining

After agreeing terms you join us, with onboarding support to settle into the field.
